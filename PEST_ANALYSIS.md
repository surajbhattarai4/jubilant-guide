# PEST Analysis - Nepali Restaurant QR Code Ordering System

## 📊 PEST Analysis Framework

PEST analysis examines **Political, Economic, Social, and Technological** factors affecting the business.

---

- ✅ **Nepal E-commerce Policy** - Digital payments are growing
- ✅ **Restaurant Licensing** - Digital systems support compliance
- ✅ **Food Safety Standards** - Menu system helps maintain records

- ⚠️ **Data Privacy Laws** - Customer data protection requirements

### Government Support

- ✅ Government incentives for restaurant digitization
- ✅ Growing mobile payment infrastructure
- ⚠️ Limited enforcement of data protection laws
- ✅ No restrictions on QR code technology

### Regulatory Environment
- ✅ Favorable for restaurant tech startups
- ✅ Government push for digital payments
- ✅ Minimal regulatory barriers

**Opportunity:** Government digitization initiatives create demand
**Risk:** Tax compliance requirements need integration

## 💰 **ECONOMIC FACTORS**

### Market Size & Growth
- **Nepal Restaurant Market:** ~₹50 billion annually
- **Growth Rate:** 8-12% annually
- **Digital Adoption:** 25-30% of restaurants (growing)
- **Target Market:** 50,000+ restaurants in Nepal

### Customer Spending Power
- ✅ Rising middle class (25-35 age group)
- ✅ Increased urban restaurant visits
- ✅ Growing smartphone adoption (98%)
- ⚠️ Price sensitivity among budget restaurants
- ⚠️ Economic fluctuations affect dining out

### Pricing Strategy
- **Basic Plan:** Free (to acquire users)
- **Professional:** ₹2,999/month (sustainable for small restaurants)
- **Enterprise:** Custom (₹10,000-50,000/month)

### Cost Analysis
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Server Hosting | ₹5,000-15,000/month |
| Database (MongoDB Atlas) | ₹2,000-5,000/month |
| Payment Gateway (Stripe/Khalti) | 2-3% per transaction |
| Support Staff | ₹80,000-200,000/month |
| Marketing | ₹20,000-50,000/month |
| **Total Monthly Cost** | **₹107,000-270,000** |

### Revenue Projection (Year 1)
- **Conservative:** 500 restaurants × ₹2,999 = ₹15 lakhs/month
- **Optimistic:** 2,000 restaurants × ₹2,999 = ₹60 lakhs/month

### Economic Challenges
- ⚠️ High churn rate (need retention strategies)
- ⚠️ Payment gateway fees reduce margins
- ⚠️ Competition from free alternatives
- ✅ High margin (SaaS typically 70-80% margin)

### Opportunities
- 📈 Growing restaurant industry
- 💳 Increasing digital payment adoption
- 🚀 Untapped market in tier-2 & tier-3 cities
- 💎 Premium features for enterprise clients

## 🔧 **TECHNOLOGICAL FACTORS**

### Current Tech Infrastructure in Nepal
- ✅ **4G/5G:** 85%+ coverage in urban areas
- ✅ **Smartphones:** 98% penetration (mainly Android)
- ✅ **Internet:** 70%+ penetration
- ⚠️ **Desktop:** Limited in restaurants
- ✅ **Payment Gateway:** Khalti, IME Pay, ESewa available

### Technology Stack Analysis

#### Frontend
- **React 18** - Industry standard, large community
- **Tailwind CSS** - Fast development, responsive
- **PWA** - Works offline, installable
- **Mobile-First** - Optimized for mobile browsers

#### Backend
- **Node.js** - Scalable, JavaScript ecosystem
- **Express** - Lightweight, flexible routing
- **MongoDB** - Flexible schema for menu variations
- **JWT Auth** - Secure, stateless authentication

#### Deployment
- **Cloud-Native:** AWS, GCP, Azure ready
- **Serverless:** Can migrate to AWS Lambda if needed
- **Containers:** Docker containerization possible
- **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions for automation

### Technology Advantages
✅ **Open Source** - No licensing costs
✅ **Scalable** - Can handle 100K+ restaurants
✅ **Fast** - Load times < 2 seconds
✅ **Secure** - Industry-standard security
✅ **Mobile-Optimized** - Perfect for Android devices
✅ **Progressive Enhancement** - Works on slow networks

### Emerging Technologies to Integrate

## 🎯 **SYNTHESIS & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS**

### SWOT Summary

#### Strengths
1. **Timely Solution** - Market is ready for digitization
2. **Affordable Pricing** - ₹2,999 is competitive
3. **Mobile-First Design** - Perfect for target market
4. **Low Setup Friction** - No technical knowledge needed
5. **Proven Tech Stack** - Industry-standard tools

#### Weaknesses
1. **Low Tech Adoption** - Conservative restaurant owners
2. **Competition** - Other POS systems exist
3. **Limited Resources** - Small team/budget
4. **Payment Gateway Dependency** - Third-party reliance
5. **Language Barrier** - Need Nepali localization

#### Opportunities
1. **Market Growth** - Restaurant industry expanding
2. **Digital Payment Growth** - Khalti, IME Pay growing
3. **Government Support** - Digital Nepal framework
4. **Unmet Needs** - No good QR menu solution yet
5. **International Expansion** - Model works for South Asia

#### Threats
1. **Competition** - Big players entering market
2. **Economic Downturn** - Affects restaurant spending
3. **Tech Skepticism** - Traditional owners resistant
4. **Payment Issues** - Khalti/ESewa outages
5. **Regulation Changes** - Unexpected compliance costs

### Market Entry Strategy

#### Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)
- Target Kathmandu restaurants
- Focus on momos/cafes (early adopters)
- Leverage word-of-mouth
- Offer free setup & support

#### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-12)
- Expand to Lalitpur, Bhaktapur
- Build restaurant community
- Add features based on feedback
- Partner with restaurant associations

#### Phase 3: Scale (Year 2)
- Expand to tier-2 cities (Pokhara, Biratnagar)
- Launch enterprise features
- International expansion (India, Bangladesh)
- Hire sales team

|------|-----------|
